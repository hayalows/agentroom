## 2. Calibration disclosure (a fit, not a validation)

- FY2026E interest income is **anchored** on 9M-2026 x 12/9 = 2,225.8m. The forward formula (364-day bill + 6.0 pts on average net loans, 91-day bill + 2.7 pts on opening liquid assets) gives 2,224.6m for FY2026E, a difference of -1.2m (-0.1%). The liquid spread was solved to land on the anchor, so this match is by construction.
- FY2026E interest expense is anchored on 9M-2026 x 12/9 = 738.4m. The cost-of-funds ratio 0.204 x an assumed 14.3% 2026 average policy rate x average funding 25,273.6m gives 737.3m (-0.1%). The 14.3% average is taken from SCB's assumptions basis and was not re-verified.
- The macro file's FY2026E bill rates (91-day 5.81%, 364-day 11.01%) are lower than rates earlier in 2026, so yields from FY2027E depend on the fitted spreads staying stable. That is untested.

## 3b. Scenario ordering and a tax sensitivity

| Year | PAT downside | PAT base | PAT upside | Ordered downside < base < upside? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FY2026E | 1,094 | 1,094 | 1,094 | yes |
| FY2027E | 1,261 | 1,125 | 1,117 | NO (inverted) |
| FY2028E | 1,548 | 1,434 | 1,429 | NO (inverted) |
| FY2029E | 1,580 | 1,753 | 1,844 | yes |
| FY2030E | 1,747 | 2,138 | 2,298 | yes |

Profit is not ordered downside < base < upside in FY2027E, FY2028E. This is a property of the shared macro file, not an error in the arithmetic: the downside has higher policy and bill rates (policy rate 14% in FY2027E-FY2028E vs 10% in base and 9% in upside), so the upside earns slightly less than the base on rate-sensitive assets, and Zenith holds about 9,000m of liquid assets that reprice with bills while the cost-of-funds ratio is 0.204 of the policy rate, so higher rates lift net interest income more than funding cost. The downside still carries a cost of risk of 4% and zero dividends, so equity is highest in the downside by FY2030E. Reviewers should challenge whether a rate-rise scenario deserves this asset sensitivity (it is the same formula as in banks/standard-chartered-ghana).

Tax sensitivity (unverified alternative): if the FY2026E tax rate were 28% (9M-2026 17.8% plus the 10.3-point step seen between 9M-2025 and FY2025), FY2026E profit after tax would be 1,194m instead of 1,094m.
