# Ecobank Ghana PLC (`ecobank-ghana`)

## Overview & Ownership
- **Legal name (BoG register):** Ecobank Ghana Plc (verified in `sector/roster-verification/verification.md`).
- **Ownership & listing status:** Publicly listed on the Ghana Stock Exchange (ticker: `EGH`). Majority-owned (68.93% of issued ordinary shares) by Ecobank Transnational Incorporated (ETI), per `EGH25` Note 3 (p. 2) and `EGH26Q3` Note 3 (p. 2).
- **Scope:** Standalone Bank (`The Bank` / separate financial statements), 31 December fiscal year, in **GHS millions** (converted from GHS thousands). Covers `FY2021–FY2025` and `9M-2026` (unaudited).

## Historical Summary (`FY2021–FY2025`, `9M-2026`)
- **Scale:** Total assets grew from GHS 17,856.7m (`FY2021`) to GHS 46,076.2m (`FY2025`) and GHS 53,807.3m (`9M-2026`). Customer deposits stood at GHS 30,397.5m (`FY2025`) and GHS 36,100.2m (`9M-2026`).
- **DDEP & Recovery:** In `FY2022`, EGH recorded a GHS 1,697.9m net impairment charge and a net loss after tax of GHS -33.5m. In `FY2023`, EGH absorbed a further GHS 1,822.2m impairment charge and a GHS 937.8m DDEP modification loss (`x_modification_loss`), yet returned to profit (PAT GHS 619.5m). Earnings rebounded strongly in `FY2024` (PAT GHS 1,687.1m) and `FY2025` (PAT GHS 1,792.4m), with `9M-2026` PAT at GHS 1,325.4m.
- **Capital & Asset Quality:** Bank-only CAR recovered from 13.49% (`FY2023`) to 17.03% (`FY2024`), 21.23% (`FY2025`), and 18.19% (`9M-2026`) — comfortably above the 13.0% BoG regulatory minimum. NPL ratio peaked at 21.27% (`FY2024`) before improving to 17.85% (`FY2025`) and 14.99% (`9M-2026`).

## Forecast Summary (`FY2026E–FY2030E`, *estimates*)
All forecasts are generated by `model.py` using `assumptions.json` and `sector/macro-assumptions/macro.csv` (`FY2026E` anchored on `9M-2026` actuals + iterative Q4 estimate; `FY2027E–FY2030E` linked to `macro.csv` nominal GDP, CPI, policy rate, T-bills, and 10Y bond yield):
- **Base case:** PAT reaches GHS `1,850.1m` (`FY2026E`, ROE `23.7%`) and `2,200.8m` (`FY2027E`) before moderating to GHS `1,910.2m` (`FY2030E`, ROE `13.7%`) as T-bill/bond yields normalize toward `9.0%–12.0%`. CAR proxy remains `18.84%–20.42%`.
- **Downside case:** Higher cost of risk (`2.0%–3.5%`) and opex inflation (`CPI + 2.5%`) compress PAT from GHS `1,807.4m` (`FY2026E`) to GHS `1,351.6m` by `FY2030E` (ROE `10.8%`; CAR proxy `18.84%–19.54%`).
- **Upside case:** Stronger loan growth (`nominal GDP + 2.5%`) and lower cost of risk (`1.0%–1.4%`) lift PAT from GHS `1,865.9m` (`FY2026E`) to GHS `2,641.9m` by `FY2030E` (ROE `18.0%`; CAR proxy `18.74%–20.46%`).

## Known Gaps & Limitations
- GSE summary statements do not split `due_from_banks` from `cash_and_bog_balances`, `loans_and_advances_gross` and `loan_loss_allowance` from `loans_and_advances_net`, total `tier1_pct` (CET1 is reported in `x_cet1_pct`), income tax vs. statutory levies, or FX currency balances (`ghs_per_usd_end` revaluation is therefore not modelled separately). Undisclosed items are marked `unavailable` in `financials.csv` and `forecast.csv` with blank values.
- Forecast `car_pct` is a proxy anchored on `9M-2026` reported `18.19%` CAR (`total_equity / (total_assets * RWA_density)`), not a prudential Tier 1 + Tier 2 calculation. The `cash_bog >= 20% * customer_deposits` check is a liquidity proxy screen rather than a regulatory BoG cedi CRR compliance proof.
