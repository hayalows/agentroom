## 2. Calibration disclosure (a fit, not a validation)

- FY2026E interest income is **anchored** on 9M-2026 x 12/9 = 877.7m. The forward formula is: 364-day bill + 6.0 pts on average net loans (252.6m for 2026), plus on opening securities of 6,376.7m a blended yield: 50.0% weight on the average of the 91- and 364-day bills and 50.0% on the government bond yield (an ASSUMED weight, not observed), plus a haircut of **-1.43 pts solved so the formula lands on the anchor**. The match is by construction. The haircut is negative because the macro blend (11.23%) is above the yield the bank earned (9.80%); the older, lower-yield holdings and any non-earning balances are not separately visible.
- FY2026E interest expense is anchored on 9M-2026 x 12/9 = 338.2m. The cost-of-funds ratio (0.239) is solved so that ratio x an assumed 14.3% 2026 average policy rate x average funding 9,896.7m gives that figure. The 14.3% average policy rate is taken from the SCB and Zenith bases and was not re-verified.
- Capital proxy: book equity less 339.9m (FY2025 book equity 1,895.7m minus regulatory capital 1,555.8m) over density 0.692 x total assets, density solved so the proxy equals the reported 16.7% at 30 Sep 2026 (FY2025 audited density 0.516). Whether the interim CAR includes the unaudited 9M profit is unknown.
- Cash is modelled at (macro CRR 20% + 11.96%) of customer deposits, the extra share solved from 30 Sep 2026 balances. The 20% is a constructed floor, not a tested statutory eligibility.
- The macro file's FY2026E bill rates (91-day 5.81%, 364-day 11.01%) are lower than rates earlier in 2026, so yields from FY2027E depend on the fitted haircut staying stable. That is untested.

## 5. Reasonableness against history (observed history, estimated forecast)

- ROE history: FY2022 5.0%, FY2023 12.9%, FY2024 11.0%, FY2025 21.5% (year-end equity). Base forecast ROE is in the table above.
- FY2025 profit after tax of 407.1m included a 118.99m reversal of expected credit losses; 9M-2026 includes a 47.1m reversal. The forecast has no further reversals, so base profit after tax in FY2026E (about 256m) is well below FY2025 (407m); the base case then grows with net interest income.
- Loan-to-deposit ratio was 19.6% at 30 Sep 2026 (net loans / customer deposits). Base loan growth in assumptions.json starts from that low base.

## 6. Known limits

- The shared macro rate paths (policy, bills, bonds) drive securities yields; deposit pricing uses a single ratio to the policy rate. There is no deposit beta, no maturity ladder and no FX translation (the statements do not give a net FX position).
- Securities are a plug (liquid assets); if loan growth exceeds deposit growth the plug falls, and a negative plug would fail check 1.
- Borrowings (1,690.6m at 30 Sep 2026, up from 807.8m at FY2025) are held flat; the reason for the increase is not stated.
- Property and equipment rose from 122.3m (30 Jun 2026) to 320.3m (30 Sep 2026); the forecast carries the September level and grows it with inflation. The cause is unknown.
- The FY2025 dividend recommended (146.5m) needed Bank of Ghana approval; 304.9m was paid in 9M-2026. Future dividends use a payout on prior-year profit with caps.
- The CAR figures are proxies; the regulatory ratio, Tier 1 and NPL are not forecast.
