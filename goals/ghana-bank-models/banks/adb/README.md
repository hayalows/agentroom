# Agricultural Development Bank PLC (`adb`)

## Overview & Ownership
- **Legal name (BoG register):** Agricultural Development Bank Plc (verified in `sector/roster-verification/verification.md`).
- **Ownership & listing status:** Publicly listed on the Ghana Stock Exchange (ticker: `ADB`), universal banking license (`ADB25` p. 3, `ADB26Q3` p. 5). Majority state-backed/recapitalised by the Government of Ghana (`ADB24` p. 2, `ADB26Q3` p. 5).
- **Scope:** Standalone Bank, 31 December fiscal year, in **GHS millions** (converted from GHS thousands). Covers `FY2021–FY2025` and `9M-2026` (unaudited).

## Historical Summary (`FY2021–FY2025`, `9M-2026`)
- **DDEP Crisis & Recapitalisation:** After `FY2022–FY2023` losses (`-371.3m` and `-828.8m` PAT) pushed total equity negative (`-102.3m` in `FY2023`) and CAR to `-22.61%`, ADB executed a Government of Ghana-backed Rights Issue in `FY2024` (raising stated capital from GHS `698.7m` to GHS `2,146.7m`) and received a GHS `850.0m` deposit for shares in `FY2025` (`ADB25` p. 3). Total equity recovered to GHS `2,477.8m` (`FY2025`) and GHS `2,761.8m` (`9M-2026`), lifting CAR to `27.17%` (`FY2025`) and `27.41%` (`9M-2026`).
- **Earnings & Asset Quality:** PAT rebounded to GHS `367.3m` (`FY2025`) and GHS `301.7m` (`9M-2026`). However, gross NPLs remain elevated at `70.53%` (`FY2025`) and `66.32%` (`9M-2026`), though supported by GHS `381.4m` (`FY2025`) and GHS `119.58m` (`9M-2026`) of loan recoveries (`ADB25` p. 3, `ADB26Q3` p. 5).

## Forecast Summary (`FY2026E–FY2030E`, *estimates*; version 2, steward claude-helper-1)
Generated by `model.py` using `assumptions.json` and `sector/macro-assumptions/macro.csv` (`FY2026E` anchored on `9M-2026` + iterative Q4; `0%` dividend payout; no automatic capital injection assumed):
- **Base case:** PAT is GHS `346.6m` (`FY2026E`), `154.9m` (`FY2027E`) and `46.5m` (`FY2028E`), then turns negative (`-154.0m` in `FY2029E`, `-308.2m` in `FY2030E`) as sovereign yields normalise against a high opex base. CAR proxy falls from `27.75%` (`FY2026E`) to `15.15%` (`FY2030E`) and stays above the `13.0%` minimum.
- **Downside case:** PAT `-62.7m` in `FY2027E` to `-683.6m` in `FY2030E`; CAR proxy is `6.04%` in `FY2029E` and `-4.46%` in `FY2030E` (`roe_pct` is `unavailable` where average equity is not positive).
- **Upside case:** PAT is positive in every year (`80.3m` in `FY2030E`); CAR proxy `25.77%` in `FY2030E`.
- Two scenario-years (downside `FY2029E` and `FY2030E`) fall below the `13.0%` minimum (`FAIL` in `checks.md`). This is a proxy result, not a prediction of a regulatory breach.
- **How much the answer depends on two assumptions** (`checks.md` section 3): base `FY2030E` PAT is `-538.8m` with no placements income and `-308.2m` with it; the capital proxy at `38%` and `48%` RWA density gives base CARs of `9.49%` and `7.51%` (gaps `410m` and `809m`), so the base result is sensitive to the RWA density, which is unknown for 2026.

## What changed in version 2 (repairs from the lead decision of 2026-11-25)
1. **Right-of-use assets** are now in `property_equipment_intangibles` once, not in `other_assets` (12 bank-period rows moved; memo row `x_right_of_use_assets`). Total assets are unchanged.
2. **Cash earns income.** Version 1 gave the combined cash bucket zero income although it holds short treasury bills (FY2025: `3,561.8m` of `9,902.4m`). The model now earns `short_bill_share_of_noncash_reserve_cash` (`54.0%`, the FY2025 observed share) x cash above the CRR proxy x the 91-day bill rate. `checks.md` section 3 shows `0%`, `27%` and `54%`; `0%` removes only the short-bill line.
3. **Source rows added** from the FY2025 annual report notes: gross loans and loan loss allowance (FY2024, FY2025), note 8 interest components, cash components, regulatory capital, tier 2, CET1 and RWA. These were transcribed from the papa-kojo-agent evidence pack; the steward did not view the pages, and the auditor's key audit matter (printed p. 38) matches `3.89bn` loans and `1.88bn` allowance.
4. **Placements income (round 2).** FY2025 placements interest (`133.1m`, note 8) was omitted. It is now earned on the non-bill share of cash above the CRR proxy at the FY2025 observed yield (`4.12%` of average other cash), held constant, with `0%`, `50%` and `100%` shown in `checks.md`. A component yield table compares FY2025 observed yields with the model; the loan yield sensitivity is `-2` / `+2` pp.
5. **Gross LDR (round 2).** Canonical `ldr_pct` is now on gross loans where gross exists (`FY2024` `35.08%`, `FY2025` `29.46%`) and `unavailable` elsewhere, including all forecast years; the net basis is in `x_net_ldr_pct` for every period.
6. **Capital stress (round 2).** `checks.md` shows the FY2030E gap to the minimum on four bases: the model proxy, a book-equity numerator at the version 1 density, and the regulatory numerator at `38%` and `48%` RWA density. Reported FY2025 CAR `27.17%` equals regulatory capital `1,038.461m` over RWA `3,821.530m`. The statutory reserve is held flat because the opening reserve is unknown; transfers at `12.5%`, `25%` and `50%` are shown as a memo (no effect on total equity).
7. **Statutory reserve (round 2b).** The reported 9M-2026 fund (`534.049m`) and year-to-date transfer (`150.857m`, `50.0%` of PAT) recovered by Codex now anchor memo rows `x_statutory_reserve` and `x_statutory_transfer` (three s34 bands in `assumptions.json`, as re-read by Codex; the steward did not read the Act). No effect on total equity or CAR.
8. **Capital proxy** now starts from FY2025 reported regulatory capital (`1,038.5m`, `41.9%` of book equity) rolled forward by the change in book equity, with RWA backed out of the reported `9M-2026` CAR. This makes losses reduce the ratio faster than the version 1 book-equity proxy.

## Known Gaps & Limitations
- Gross loans and the loan loss allowance exist for `FY2024` and `FY2025` only (annual report note 21); they are `unavailable` for `FY2021–FY2023` and `9M-2026`. `due_to_banks` and total `tier1_pct` (CET1 is reported in `x_cet1_pct`); `due_from_banks` is only split in `FY2021–FY2022` (`unavailable` in `FY2023–9M-2026`).
- Forecast `car_pct` is a proxy (see version 2 item 4), not prudential RWA/capital, and `cash_bog >= 20% * customer_deposits` is a liquidity proxy screen (not regulatory BoG cedi CRR compliance).
- Minor 1–2 thousand (`0.001–0.002` GHS m) printed rounding differences in `ADB22` equity sum (`FY2022`), `ADB25` asset sum (`FY2025`), and `ADB26Q3` asset sum/PAT (`9M-2026`) are preserved as reported without plugging.
- Still open: the loan yield is applied to net loans (observed FY2025 loan interest over average net loans was `24.5%`, over gross `13.8%`), interest on placements and nostro is not modelled separately, and the 9M-2026 short-bill share is unknown. Regulatory NPL ratios differ by page (`70.53%` or `75.26%` for FY2025/FY2024 summary vs `75.36%` in note 5); `npl_pct` keeps the summary figure.
