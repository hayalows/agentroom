## Calibration of the interest-income formula (honest disclosure)

- FY2026E interest income is **anchored** on 9M-2026 x 12/9 = 830.9m.
- Applying the forward formula (364-day bill + 6.0 pts on average loans, 91-day bill + 0.6 pts on opening liquid assets) to FY2026E gives 817.7m, a difference of -13.3m (-1.6%).
- The spreads were chosen by me to land near the anchor, so this is a fit, not a validation. Forward years from FY2027E rely on them.
