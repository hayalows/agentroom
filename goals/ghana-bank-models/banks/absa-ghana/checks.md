## 2. Calibration disclosure (a fit, not a validation)

- FY2026E interest income is **anchored** on 9M-2026 x 12/9 = 3,112.3m. The forward formula is: 364-day bill + 6.0 pts on average net loans (1,830.6m for 2026), plus on opening securities of 9,279.9m a blended yield: 50.0% weight on the average of the 91- and 364-day bills and 50.0% on the government bond yield (an ASSUMED weight, not observed), plus a haircut of **+2.58 pts solved so the formula lands on the anchor**. The match is by construction. The haircut is positive here: the pool earned more than the macro blend (11.23%), which means either the pool yields more than assumed or, more likely, the 364-day bill + 6 points understates what Absa earns on loans (its loan book is 11.5bn, so a 1 point loan-yield error is about 0.1bn). The split between loans and the pool cannot be identified from the statements; only the total is fitted.
- FY2026E interest expense is anchored on 9M-2026 x 12/9 = 569.0m. The cost-of-funds ratio (0.155) is solved so that ratio x an assumed 14.3% 2026 average policy rate x average funding 25,641.2m gives that figure. The 14.3% average policy rate is taken from the SCB and Zenith bases and was not re-verified.
- Capital proxy: the reported 19.06% CAR at 30 Sep 2026 scaled by equity / total assets relative to its 30 Sep 2026 level (16.34%). It assumes risk-weighted assets grow with total assets and no change in regulatory deductions. No annual CAR or RWA history exists in the source pack, so this is weaker than the Zenith and UBA proxies.
- Cash is modelled at (macro CRR 20% + 23.39%) of customer deposits, the extra share solved from 30 Sep 2026 balances. The 20% is a constructed floor, not a tested statutory eligibility.
- The macro file's FY2026E bill rates (91-day 5.81%, 364-day 11.01%) are lower than rates earlier in 2026, so yields from FY2027E depend on the fitted haircut staying stable. That is untested.

## 6. Known limits

- The shared macro rate paths (policy, bills, bonds) drive securities yields; deposit pricing uses a single ratio to the policy rate. There is no deposit beta, no maturity ladder and no FX translation (the statements do not give a net FX position).
- Securities are a plug (liquid assets); if loan growth exceeds deposit growth the plug falls, and a negative plug would fail check 1.
- Borrowings (5,754.5m at 30 Sep 2026, up from 4,077.6m at FY2025) are held flat; terms were not read.
- Trading assets (3,637m) are held flat inside other assets; the history is a codex transcription the owner could not re-read (sources.md).
- 1,115m of dividends was paid in 9M-2026; later dividends use a payout on prior-year profit with caps.
- The CAR figures are proxies; the regulatory ratio, Tier 1 and NPL are not forecast.
