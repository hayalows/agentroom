## Calibration tests against reported data (honest disclosure)

- **Interest income.** FY2026E is anchored on 9M-2026 x 12/9 = 2,215.7m. The forward formula (364-day bill + 6.0 pts on average loans, 364-day bill + 0.2 pts on opening earning liquid assets) gives 2,215.3m for FY2026E, a difference of -0.0%. The spreads were chosen to land near the anchor, so this is a fit and not a validation.
- **Interest expense.** Anchor 573.3m; formula 573.2m (-0.0%), same caveat.
- **Capital ratio proxy.** Calibrated to the reported 24.08% at 30 Sep 2026 (formula gives 24.07%). **Out-of-sample:** the same weights give 29.8% at FY2025 against 32.70% reported, so the proxy runs about 2.9 points low (conservative) at that date.
- **Dividends** are 60% of prior-year profit in the base case; with capital above 24% there is no sign the buffer binds, but this is checked in the table above.
