# Fidelity Bank Ghana Limited (`fidelity-bank-ghana`) — Sources

**Entity modeled:** Fidelity Bank Ghana LTD ("The Bank", standalone separate financial statements per `MODEL-SPEC.md`).
**Currency & Units:** Ghana Cedis (`GHS`), reported in `GHS '000` in all primary filings and converted to `GHS millions` (`÷ 1,000`).
**Listing & Ownership Status:** Unlisted private/public-interest licensed commercial bank (not listed on the Ghana Stock Exchange). Wholly owns subsidiary Fidelity Securities Limited (`FSL`) and foreign subsidiary Fidelity Bank (UK) / Sierra Leone? — see Note 1 / Shareholding notes in `FBL-Annual-Report-2025.pdf`.
