# CalBank PLC (`calbank`)

## Overview & Ownership
- **Legal name (BoG register):** CalBank PLC (verified in `sector/roster-verification/verification.md`).
- **Ownership & listing status:** Publicly listed on the Ghana Stock Exchange (ticker: `CAL`; IPO 2004, `CAL26Q3` p. 6). Universal banking license since 2004.
- **Scope:** Standalone Bank (`The Bank` / separate financial statements), 31 December fiscal year, in **GHS millions** (converted from GHS thousands). Covers `FY2021–FY2025` and `9M-2026` (unaudited).

## Historical Summary (`FY2021–FY2025`, `9M-2026`)
- **DDEP Crisis & Negative Equity (`FY2022–FY2023`):** CalBank recorded heavy credit and investment impairments during Ghana's DDEP: PAT fell to GHS `-815.2m` (`FY2022`) and GHS `-680.3m` (`FY2023`), pushing total equity negative to GHS `-184.9m` and regulatory CAR to `-9.9%` (`FY2023`) under BoG's 10% temporary CAR forbearance (`CAL24` p. 119).
- **Recapitalisation & Turnaround (`FY2024–9M-2026`):** Under a BoG-approved capital restoration plan, CalBank raised stated capital from GHS `400.0m` (`FY2023`) to GHS `545.8m` (`FY2024`) and GHS `1,421.5m` (`FY2025`), returning to positive equity (GHS `1,528.4m` in `FY2025`, GHS `1,850.1m` in `9M-2026`) and CAR of `18.87%` (`FY2025`) and `18.4%` (`9M-2026`).
- **Asset Quality & Balance Sheet:** Following GHS `997.6m` of loan write-offs in `FY2025`, gross NPLs fell from `47.51%` (`FY2024`) to `17.04%` (`FY2025`) and `8.5%` (`9M-2026`, meeting BoG's 10% Dec 2026 NPL directive). Net loans doubled from GHS `1,070.9m` (`FY2025`) to GHS `2,054.2m` (`9M-2026`).

## Known Gaps & Limitations
- `9M-2026` Q3 summary does not disclose `loans_and_advances_gross`, `loan_loss_allowance`, or total `tier1_pct` (CET1 `16.4%` is recorded in `x_cet1_pct`); all undisclosed items are marked `unavailable` with blank values.
- `due_from_banks` is not split from `cash_and_bog_balances` on the balance sheet face (`unavailable`). `cash_bog >= 20% * customer_deposits` is a liquidity proxy screen, not regulatory BoG cedi CRR compliance.
- Forecast `car_pct` is a proxy calibrated to `9M-2026` reported `18.4%` CAR, not a prudential Tier 1 + Tier 2 calculation.
- Historical `roe_pct` for `FY2023–FY2024` is marked `unavailable` because average equity was negative/near zero.
