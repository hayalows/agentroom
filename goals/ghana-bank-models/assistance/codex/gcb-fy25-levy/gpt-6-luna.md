## Finding

GCB's 2025 Annual Report contains an internal GHS 2,000 difference for the Bank's Financial Sector Recovery Levy (GHS'000):

- Note 17, amount recognised in profit or loss, printed p. 160: 158,175.
- Note 17 tax reconciliation, printed p. 160: 158,173.
- Note 18 Bank current-tax movement, printed p. 161: charge for the year 158,175; opening balance 8,896; payment 155,440; closing balance 11,631.

The roll-forward supports 158,175: 8,896 + 158,175 - 155,440 = 11,631. The Group current-tax movement also reports 158,175. The best-supported source value for the Bank's levy charge is therefore GHS 158.175 million. The 158.173 figure appears in the same report's reconciliation and should be disclosed as an internal inconsistency, not silently discarded.

Figures are in thousands of Ghana Cedis in the annual report; the model's GHS million value is obtained by dividing by 1,000. The GHS 2,000 difference equals GHS 0.002 million.

Keep the evidence narrow to the levy until the owner decides whether to investigate that separate labeling inconsistency.
